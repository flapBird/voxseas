The percentages refer to **each reroll paid for with Crystals**. The **67.5%** total for Epic is split equally between Wildborn, Tideborn and Angelborn. Look for Wildborn’s passive in the in-game description if it becomes available.

## How to reroll your race

1. Open **Character**.
2. Find the portrait of your current race.
3. Select the circular arrow next to the portrait.
4. Check the odds and price on the screen.
5. Confirm payment of **500 Crystals** or **50 Robux**.

Each reroll is random. For early quests, you can make progress with your current race by following the story and investing in the weapon and stats you use.

## Which race suits your build?

### Wyrmborn for combat

**Wyrmborn** provides **5% more damage** and **5% more maximum health**. Those bonuses apply across quests, farming and boss fights. The health bonus scales with maximum health.

### Angelborn for recovery

**Angelborn** improves health regeneration by **5%**, which helps between fights and during longer encounters.

### Tideborn for underwater exploration

**Tideborn** allows underwater breathing, improves underwater vision and increases underwater swimming speed by **25%**. The passive is useful on diving and exploration routes.

### Manborn and Wildborn

**Manborn** has no passive bonus in the documented menu. **Wildborn** has a listed rarity and reroll chance. Check the in-game description for details on the bonus.
