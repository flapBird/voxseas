## Finding Duskfang Reaper Veyrn

Sail to [Forbidden Island](/en/map/forbidden-island/). The [official Veyrn card](https://trello.com/c/vhG7qciP) points to this island, and [his key card](https://trello.com/c/5JQ2Fqdr) also directs players to the Forbidden Island NPC.

Use Darius’s camp as a landmark on your first visit. If you are there for the story, continue the current objective and return to the Room Keeper when you are ready for the challenge.

## Joining Veyrn’s room

Select **Show me the Boss Rooms** at the Room Keeper, choose **Create Room** and select Veyrn. The documented **Normal** screen requires **level 100** from each player and accepts **1 to 8 players**. The host provides the entry item.

Check the difficulty and gather the party before spending the key. On your first attempt, check which attacks you can use while your primary move recharges. Having time to reposition helps you stay active through the fight.

## Death Fangs and other rewards

The board lists **Wolf Leather**, **Wolf Skull**, chest keys and the **Death Fangs** weapon. Its other gear includes **Howling Moon Talisman**, **Moonfang Belt**, **Moonfang Boots**, **Crimson Howl Cape**, **Crimson Howl Hood** and **Moonfang Ring**.

Check the room’s rewards before farming a specific piece. If you get a recipe, see which ingredients you still need at the **Forge**. For the room procedure and build preparation, see the [boss directory](/en/bosses/) and the [weapons guide](/en/weapons/).
