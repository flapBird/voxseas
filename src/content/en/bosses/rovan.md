## Finding Rovan

The [Bandit King’s official card](https://trello.com/c/77wmCKcJ) points to **Solmar Island**, the starting area. [Rovan’s key card](https://trello.com/c/oUkHiZGm) also directs you to an NPC on Solmar. Use the [map guide](/en/map/) to get there and agree on a meeting point before creating the room.

## Preparing for the Boss Room

At the **Room Keeper**, select **Show me the Boss Rooms**, open **Create Room** and check Rovan’s difficulty options. The documented Normal screen allows **1 to 8 players** and requires **level 100**. The host provides the entry item. Match the key in the inventory to the item shown on the room screen before confirming.

For a private room, make sure the group has found the NPC and meets the level requirement. Bring a primary attack and a way to move away between uses. Your weapon or fruit mastery determines which moves will be available while that attack recharges.

## Rewards worth checking

The Trello lists **Tattered Bandana**, **Outlaw’s Seal** and **Forge Ember**, as well as chest keys and six Outlaw pieces. The documented Normal screen shows three rewards as **recipes**.

- Outlaw’s Skull Pendant
- Outlaw’s Belt
- Outlaw’s Warboots

Before repeating the room, check which recipes you have learned and which ingredients you still need at the **Forge**. Use the selected difficulty’s reward screen to check whether it offers the equipment you are after.

For room setup and other encounters, see the [boss directory](/en/bosses/). If your next farming target is a weapon, see [Veyrn’s Death Fangs guide](/en/bosses/veyrn/).
