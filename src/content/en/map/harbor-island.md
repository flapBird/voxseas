## Getting to Harbor Island

Equip the **Navigator’s Map**, open **WORLD** and look for Harbor Island. Use the **YOU** marker and compass to plot the crossing. The [Vox Seas map guide](/en/map/) explains how to get a map and boat from the opening quests. Harbor is a service stop for players looking to roll a fruit or buy an early weapon.

## Vox Gacha prices on Harbor Island

The [official Vox Gacha card](https://trello.com/c/NnwXCuLr) lists a base price of **30 Gold per character level**, with a **3,000 Gold minimum**.

| Character level | Base price |
| --- | --- |
| 1 to 100 | 3,000 Gold |
| 200 | 6,000 Gold |
| 500 | 15,000 Gold |

Talk to the NPC, choose **Spin the roulette** and check the quote before selecting **Roll**. There is a two-hour cooldown between rolls. If the NPC shows a timer, use the remaining time to run another quest or check **Temp Fruits** stock. The [gacha guide](/en/fruit-gacha/) covers rolling and replacing your current fruit.

## What the Weapons Dealer sells

The [Weapons Dealer card](https://trello.com/c/VNtLDmlf) lists a **Katana** for **300 Gold** and a **Flintlock** for **750 Gold** at Harbor. The Katana is a starting sword and the Flintlock is the shop’s firearm option. Check what you already own first, especially if you claimed Hango’s Cutlass.

If you sailed here for a fruit, set aside the price of the roll before shopping. Compare starting moves and mastery in the [weapons guide](/en/weapons/).
