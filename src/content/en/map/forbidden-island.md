## Sailing from Solmar

Follow **Supplies for the Crossing** to the shop behind Hale. Make sure the **Boat**, **Shipwright’s Hammer** and **Navigator’s Map** are in your inventory. Use the boat in open water, then open **WORLD** to find Forbidden Island. Compare the map heading with the quest marker before you cross. The [official island card](https://trello.com/c/5JAuAVUE) lists it as a **level 10–45** area.

## Darius’s camp and the Witch House

The camp is a landmark for Darius’s quests. Set your return point when you find the spawn service. Do this before continuing to Morgana at the **Witch House** so you have a shorter trip back if a fight ends away from your boat.

Follow your active story objective when choosing between exploring and progressing the quest. Once you have the **Sacred Flower**, sail back to the Solmar dock and find **Beatriz**. After the hand-in, speak to Hango at the Elder’s House and choose **Accept the Fruit** when offered. The [beginner guide](/en/beginner/) walks through the sequence on both islands.

## Where Veyrn fits into the route

The [Veyrn guide](/en/bosses/veyrn/) covers the rewards you can plan a return trip for and what to check on the room screen. Use the island’s level range to plan ordinary quests. Check the level and key in **Boss Rooms** when returning for a group challenge.

For the crossing back or your next destination, use the [island map and directory](/en/map/).
