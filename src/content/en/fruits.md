The [official Vox Seas Trello](https://trello.com/b/qGXeMuSt/vox-seas-official-trello) calls the magma fruit [Vulcanic](/en/fruits/vulcanic/). Older rankings may spell it **Volcanic**.

## How to get fruits

### Earn Hango’s fruit

Finish the Forbidden Island story, hand the **Sacred Flower** to Beatriz at Solmar’s dock, then return to Hango at the Elder’s House. Choose **Accept the Fruit** when he offers the story reward.

### Buy a temporary fruit

Open **Shop → Temp Fruits** to check what you can buy with Gold. The confirmation tells you which equipped ability the purchase will replace. **Time for New Offers** shows when the shop refreshes.

Permanent fruit cards appear under **Perm Fruits** and cost Robux. Once you own a card, use **Equip** to select it.

### Use the Vox Gacha

The NPC is on [Harbor Island](/en/map/harbor-island/) and inside the restaurant on **Wandering Bastille**. Each roll returns a random fruit, with a two-hour cooldown between rolls. The [Vox Gacha guide](/en/fruit-gacha/) covers cost, location and when to use the shop.

## How to use fruits and build mastery

After equipping a fruit, put the ability on your bar and follow the controls in the game. See [Thorn’s four moves](/en/fruits/thorn/) for an example of how mastery progresses.

Use fruit abilities against enemies to develop mastery. Group enemies before using area attacks and move while your abilities recharge.

Open the **Fruitdex** from the sidebar to track discoveries and mastery. Use **Claim** on an unlocked entry to collect the reward shown there. The documented Thorn entry shows 250 Gold.

## Removing an equipped fruit

Visit the **Fruit Remover** on the ground floor of the Harbor Island hotel. Removal costs **10,000 Gold** and preserves mastery. The [November 17 notes](https://trello.com/c/q0rcGnIp) say numbered fruits return to your inventory and fruits bought with Robux can be equipped again.

## How Logia protection works

In the **November 15, 2026** balance update, basic attacks and ordinary abilities can pass through a player with Logia protection when both conditions apply.

- Your level is at least **10 above** the enemy’s.
- The enemy has no **Vox Armor**.

The [November 18 update](https://trello.com/c/q0rcGnIp) gives enemies at level 325 and above Vox Armor. Bosses, opponents with Vox Armor and enemies close to your level can still require a different approach. For role comparisons, see the [Legacy fruit tier list](/en/tier-list/).
