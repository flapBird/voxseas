## How to get fruits

### Earn Hango’s fruit

Finish the Forbidden Island story, hand the **Sacred Flower** to Beatriz at Solmar’s dock, then return to Hango at the Elder’s House. Choose **Accept the Fruit** when he offers the story reward.

### Buy a temporary fruit

Open **Shop → Temp Fruits** to check what you can buy with Gold. The confirmation tells you which equipped ability the purchase will replace. **Time for New Offers** shows when the shop refreshes.

Permanent fruit cards appear under **Perm Fruits** and cost Robux. Once you own a card, use **Equip** to select it.

### Use the Vox Gacha

The NPC is on **Harbor Island** and inside the restaurant on **Wandering Bastille**. Each roll returns a random fruit, with a two-hour cooldown between rolls. The [Vox Gacha guide](/en/fruit-gacha/) covers cost, location and when to use the shop.

## How to use fruits and build mastery

After equipping a fruit, put the ability on your bar and follow the controls in the game. With Thorn, **Z** uses Thorn Slash. **Thorn Rain** unlocks at mastery 10 and **Thorn Bullet** at mastery 15 in the example.

Use fruit abilities against enemies to develop mastery. Group enemies before using area attacks and move while your abilities recharge.

Open the **Fruitdex** from the sidebar to track discoveries and mastery. Use **Claim** on an unlocked entry to collect the reward shown there. The documented Thorn entry shows 250 Gold.

## How Logia protection works

In the **November 15, 2026** balance update, basic attacks and ordinary abilities can pass through a player with Logia protection when both conditions apply.

- Your level is at least **10 above** the enemy’s.
- The enemy has no **Vox Armor**.

Bosses, opponents with Vox Armor and enemies close to your level can still require a different approach. For role comparisons, see the [Legacy fruit tier list](/en/tier-list/).
