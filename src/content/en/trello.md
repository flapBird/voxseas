## Is there a Vox Seas Trello board?

Our **November 16, 2026** review did not verify a public board maintained by the official team. We therefore do not present a third-party board or an invented address as the official Vox Seas Trello.

A board could be private, discontinued or simply absent. The lack of a confirmed link here does not prove any one of those possibilities. It means the safer answer is to explain how to find trustworthy information.

## How to evaluate a board you find in search

Before relying on a Trello board, check four things:

1. **Origin:** was the URL posted on the game page, developer group or an official social channel?
2. **Activity:** do recent cards and dates match the current release?
3. **Accuracy:** do prices, abilities and item names match the game interface?
4. **Safety:** viewing a public board should not require a Roblox password, extension or suspicious download.

If the source cannot be verified, treat the board as community-maintained material rather than official documentation.

## Where to track official updates

Start with the [Vox Seas page on Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/104067066727140). Its description, developer identity and published social links provide the strongest starting point.

## A practical update routine

Even without an official Trello, a simple routine keeps your information current:

| When | What to check |
|---|---|
| Before playing | Codes and XP boosts for the session |
| After an update | Patch notes, prices, abilities and entry requirements |
| Before changing builds | Fruit, race, weapon and stat compatibility |
| Before following an old guide | Publication date and Legacy labels |

This prevents two common mistakes: spending resources from an outdated ranking and assuming that every wiki-style page represents the development team.
