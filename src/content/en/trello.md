## Is there a Vox Seas Trello board?

Open the [official Vox Seas Trello](https://trello.com/b/qGXeMuSt/vox-seas-official-trello). The [Social Media - Links card](https://trello.com/c/btXX8GT0) points to the Roblox community that publishes the game. The board covers Dawn of Legends, including ability demonstrations and reward cards.

## Finding the right card

| List | What to look up |
| --- | --- |
| Vox Fruits | Fruit rarity, move names and mastery milestones |
| Islands | The world map, regions and level ranges |
| Bosses and Keys | Boss rewards and where to use entry keys |
| Races | Passive effects for all five races |
| Info | Codes, controls and developer links |

Use the in-game menu to check names and moves when you compare old guides with the current release. A Legacy guide may use a different name for a move or item. Start with the [Fruitdex](/en/fruits/) or the [island map](/en/map/) if you already know the name you need.

## Where to track official updates

Check the [Codes card](https://trello.com/c/0DaBs76i) for rewards and the [official Roblox page](https://www.roblox.com/games/104067066727140) to join the current game. Our [code list](/en/codes/) shows the review date and redemption steps. For community announcements, see the [Discord guide](/en/discord/).
