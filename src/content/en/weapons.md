Use the [island map](/en/map/) to find the right dealer. Check the in-game price before you buy.

## Early weapons

### Cutlass

Hango gives you a **Cutlass** after the Marine investigation on Solmar. Claim it through the story to save the 1,500 Gold it costs at the Arms Dealer. The documented **Moon Fang Slash** requirement is mastery 25.

### Katana

A **Katana** costs **300 Gold** and gives you **Air Wave** immediately. It is a low-cost choice if you want a weapon before Hango’s reward.

### Flintlock

The **Flintlock** is an early ranged option for **750 Gold**. Its Z ability, **Multiple Shots**, unlocks at mastery 25 in the documented list.

## Dual Katana, Slingshot and Musket

**Dual Katana** costs 5,000 Gold and uses two blades with **Phantom Slash**. Look for it when you reach Circus Town.

**Slingshot** costs 10,000 Gold. Its **Prison Shot** traps the target at the impact point. The documented Z ability costs 35 energy and has a 12-second cooldown.

**Musket** costs 25,000 Gold. Hold the ability input to charge **Overcharge Strike**. Compare its charged shot with what you can accomplish using the Slingshot before spending the extra 15,000 Gold.

## How to build weapon mastery

Fight with the weapon to increase its mastery and unlock abilities. The documented milestones are:

| Weapon | Z unlock | X unlock |
|---|---|---|
| Katana | Available on purchase | 25 |
| Cutlass and Dual Katana | 25 | 75 |
| Flintlock | 25 | 50 |
| Slingshot and Musket | 25 | 75 |

Train on enemies you can defeat reliably. Before joining a group fight, check the [Boss Room requirements](/en/bosses/#how-boss-rooms-work).

## Weapons sold for Robux

When stocked, **SHOP → PRODUCTS** lists **Abyssal Blade** for 1,000 Robux and **Monarch Daggers** for 5,000 Robux. The older Dark Blade, Cérberus Sword and Monarch Daggers passes were listed as off sale in Legacy.

Check the current shop card for availability. If you need resources for regular progression, check the [code list](/en/codes/) for rewards.
