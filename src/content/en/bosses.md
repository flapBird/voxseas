## How Boss Rooms work

The [Rovan](/en/bosses/rovan/) and [Veyrn](/en/bosses/veyrn/) challenge rooms accept **1 to 8 players**. To open a Normal room, each player needs **level 100** and the host needs a **Normal Key**.

Meet at the **Room Keeper** and follow these steps.

1. Select **Show me the Boss Rooms**.
2. Choose **Create Room**.
3. Pick a difficulty.
4. Set access to **Public** or **Private**.
5. Set the party size and confirm.

The host’s entry item is consumed. Players defeated during the encounter wait until it ends, so prepare before entering.

## Rovan rewards

On Normal difficulty, the **Outlaw’s Skull Pendant**, **Belt** and **Warboots** recipes each have a listed **16.48%** drop chance.

Take these recipes to the **Forge** to craft the accessories. Check the materials you still need before repeating the room. For Veyrn and other bosses, use the reward screen to check what your current room can drop.

## Frost Island story rewards

After reporting on Ice King, defeat the **Ice Golem** for the **Royal Chest Key**. Open the marked chest in the hall with the key to get the **Yeti Bell**.

After defeating **Yeti**, go up to it, select **E → Talk**, then choose **Collect a blood sample** to get the **Yeti Blood** quest item. These rewards come from the story steps.

## How to prepare for a fight

Match stats to the damage you use. **Sword** supports sword attacks, **Vox Fruit** supports fruit abilities and **Strength** supports fighting styles. **Endurance** adds health and stamina. Compare area damage, control and mobility in the [Legacy tier list](/en/tier-list/).

Train your main weapon to unlock more abilities before the fight. See the [weapon mastery milestones](/en/weapons/#how-to-build-weapon-mastery).

Use a private room if you want to coordinate a group. Before the host uses the key, make sure everyone is near the Room Keeper, meets the level requirement and knows the difficulty. Use the [map](/en/map/) to plan where to meet.
