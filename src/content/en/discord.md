## Use the official Roblox page as your starting point

Open the [official Vox Seas page on Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/104067066727140) and look for developer-published **social links**. This is safer than copying an invite from an old video description, comment or unofficial wiki.

If the link is missing, check:

- whether you are signed in to the intended account;
- whether the account meets Roblox age and verification requirements;
- whether privacy settings affect social-link visibility;
- whether the developer has posted community information in the description or official group.

> A large member count does not prove that a server is official. The publisher of the invitation matters more than its name or popularity.

## Why there is no direct invite button on this page

Discord invitations can expire, be replaced or circulate outside their intended community. During this review, we could not independently verify a current public invitation as the official one.

Rather than filling the page with a questionable link, we keep the route through the game page. That also prevents an expired invitation from being presented as verified months later.

## Channels worth checking

Once inside the official community, read channel descriptions and pinned messages. Useful areas commonly include:

- **announcements** for major news and community milestones;
- **updates, changelog or patch notes** for balance and mechanic changes;
- **codes** for new rewards and expiry notices;
- **sneak peeks** for unfinished content that may still change;
- **support** for account-safe troubleshooting and reporting instructions.

Actual channel names may differ. Read the server rules before posting, and do not use support channels to demand codes.

## Verify codes before using them

Check the message date and confirm that the code came from a team account with an appropriate server role. Then redeem it through the game's own **Menu → Settings → CODES** field.

Our [Vox Seas codes page](/en/codes/) records a review date and known rewards. A screenshot without source or date is not enough to prove that a code still works.

## Stay safe in the community

Never share your password, session cookie, verification code or payment details. A legitimate moderator does not need your password to grant a reward.

Be cautious with direct messages offering Robux, permanent fruits or early access. Do not run downloaded files, install “redeem plugins” or authorize unrelated applications.
