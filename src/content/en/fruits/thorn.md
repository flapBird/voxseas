## Thorn moves by mastery

| Mastery | Move |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Thorn Slash |
| 10 | Thorn Rain |
| 15 | Thorn Bullet |
| 25 | Thorn Ball |

Equip Thorn on your bar and use **Z** for Thorn Slash as shown in the interface. The [official Thorn card](https://trello.com/c/ca82xvrW) demonstrates all four moves. Check the in-game bar for the controls as each new move becomes available.

## How to train Thorn on your first quests

Character level and fruit mastery progress separately. Use Thorn in combat to reach the mastery milestones above. Start with one ordinary enemy to see where Thorn Slash lands. After you unlock Thorn Rain, try gathering a small group before casting the area attack. Pay attention to how quickly you clear the group and how long you wait before the move becomes available again.

Once Thorn Bullet unlocks, see how far away you can land it consistently. Keep it ready for enemies that separate or start closing the gap again. At mastery 25, try Thorn Ball on the same route to see which encounter is easiest to clear with it. The [beginner route](/en/beginner/) has early encounters where you can practice this sequence.

## Checking the discovery reward

Open the **Fruitdex** from the sidebar and select Thorn to see your unlocked abilities. A documented discovery screen shows **250 Gold** under **Claim**. Use the value on your own Fruitdex entry to see what you can collect.

If you are looking for Thorn, check the current **Temp Fruits** stock. For a random roll, see the [Vox Gacha guide](/en/fruit-gacha/). The [fruit directory](/en/fruits/) lets you compare abilities before replacing a fruit you have already trained.
