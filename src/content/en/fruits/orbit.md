## Orbit mastery unlocks

| Mastery | Move |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Gravity Missiles |
| 75 | Gravity Push |
| 150 | Gravity Manipulation |
| 225 | Meteor Rain |
| 300 | Gravity Collapse |

This is the move progression on the [official Orbit card](https://trello.com/c/ubbr96x1). The fruit menu shows your current mastery and controls. Use that screen to check your next unlock.

## How to build an Orbit rotation

Start with **Gravity Missiles** against ordinary enemies and see how its range fits the route you run. Once **Gravity Push** unlocks, watch where enemies end up after the hit. Pushing targets away can give you room to recover and can scatter a group you just brought together. Try both situations before building your routine around the move.

Try **Gravity Manipulation** and **Meteor Rain** on groups you already know. Notice how much space each move covers and when you get better results by waiting for targets to come closer. At mastery 300, test **Gravity Collapse** in the same encounter to see where it improves your rotation.
