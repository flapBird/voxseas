## How to identify Vulcanic

Look up **Vulcanic** in the Fruitdex. That is the spelling on the [official fruit card](https://trello.com/c/Irp1rciT). If a ranking uses Volcanic or Magma, check its publication date and find the Vulcanic card in your game before spending Gold on the strength of an old ranking.

## Magma moves and mastery

| Mastery | Trello move label |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Magma |
| 75 | Magma |
| 150 | Magma |
| 225 | Volcanic |
| 300 | Magma |

The Trello repeats **Magma** across four milestones. Compare the official demonstrations by mastery level and read the full name shown on your move bar. The next unlock gives you a concrete training goal beyond a placement in a [Legacy tier list](/en/tier-list/).

## When the Logia passive helps

The official card shows Vulcanic’s Logia passive. The [Fruitdex guide explains the documented protection rules](/en/fruits/#how-logia-protection-works), including the gap between your level and the target’s level and the effect of Vox Armor. Try it against a regular enemy you can handle safely. Watch what happens with basic attacks before relying on the passive against groups or bosses.

## Choosing between the shop and gacha

Check **Shop → Temp Fruits** if you want to select the result. When Vulcanic appears in stock, read the price and replacement notice before you confirm. The [Vox Gacha](/en/fruit-gacha/) charges per roll and gives a random fruit. If you already have a fruit with high mastery, compare what Vulcanic offers at the first few milestones before switching.
