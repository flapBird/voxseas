## Eclipse mastery unlocks

| Fruit mastery | Move |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Dark Blade |
| 75 | Dark Materialization |
| 150 | Dark Impact |
| 225 | Abyssal Orb |
| 300 | Dark Cataclysm |

## Using Abyssal Orb twice

At mastery 225, cast Abyssal Orb and activate the same ability again. The [official Eclipse card](https://trello.com/c/rejVIK01) shows each input in a separate clip. Look for **Second Click of the Ability** just below the first Abyssal Orb clip to see the follow-up.

## Choosing Eclipse

Use the [Fruitdex](/en/fruits/) to compare Eclipse with other Legendary fruits such as Mirror, Frozen and Shine. The [Legacy tier list](/en/tier-list/) places Eclipse in S tier for its ability to pull targets together in that version. In Dawn of Legends, factor in your current mastery when choosing a move: at mastery 150 through 224, for example, you can use Dark Impact while working toward Abyssal Orb.
