## Start on Solmar

Select **START GAME** and follow the yellow marker to the **Elder’s House**. Talk to Hango and follow the festival quests through the Marine investigation. When you return, choose **Take the Cutlass** when Hango offers the sword.

Check the [code list and redemption steps](/en/codes/#redeem) before starting a quest chain. If you redeem a timed XP boost, use it on quests and fights you can already access.

## Prepare your first boat

When **Supplies for the Crossing** starts, find the shop behind Hale on Solmar.

1. Claim the **Boat**, **Shipwright’s Hammer** and **Navigator’s Map** individually.
2. Equip the items from your inventory and use the boat in a clear stretch of water.
3. Equip the map and open **WORLD**. The green **YOU** marker shows your position.
4. Follow the quest marker to **Forbidden Island**. Set your spawn at the camp before continuing.

Look up other destinations and services in the [island map](/en/map/#islands-and-destinations).

## Get your first fruit

Follow the Forbidden Island story until you obtain the **Sacred Flower**. Give it to **Beatriz** at the Solmar dock, then return to the Elder’s House. Choose **Accept the Fruit** when Hango offers the reward.

To try another power later, compare the [fruit list and abilities](/en/fruits/#fruit-list-and-abilities). Check **Temp Fruits** in the shop or look for the [Vox Gacha](/en/fruit-gacha/) on Harbor Island or at the Wandering Bastille restaurant.

## Develop your first build

Use **Sword** when the Cutlass is your main source of damage. Use **Vox Fruit** for fruit abilities or **Strength** for fighting styles. **Endurance** adds health and stamina.

Use the Cutlass in early quests to build mastery. Before spending Gold on another sword, compare [weapon prices and mastery milestones](/en/weapons/#how-to-build-weapon-mastery). The story returns to Hango before continuing to Circus Town. When you are ready for a group encounter, check the [Boss Room requirements](/en/bosses/#how-boss-rooms-work).

Use the [official Roblox game page](https://www.roblox.com/games/104067066727140) and the [island map](/en/map/) to plan your next voyage.
