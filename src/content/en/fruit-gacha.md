## Where to roll fruits

| Location | How to get there |
|---|---|
| Harbor Island | Find the Vox Gacha NPC on the island |
| Wandering Bastille | Enter the restaurant through the ground-floor double doors |

Wandering Bastille is a restaurant ship. Approach by boat, board from the side and head inside. Use the [island directory](/en/map/#islands-and-destinations) to look up services at both locations.

## How to use the Vox Gacha

1. Bring enough Gold to cover the quoted price.
2. Talk to the **Vox Gacha** NPC.
3. Choose **Spin the roulette**.
4. Check the price and select **Roll**.
5. Review the fruit you received and equip it if you want to replace your current one.

Each attempt gives a random fruit. The NPC applies a **two-hour cooldown** after the roll. If you want to keep your current ability, read the replacement notice before equipping the new fruit.

## How much does a roll cost?

Check the quote in the dialogue. In one documented example, a roll cost **5,000 Gold**, or **3,750 Gold** with an active Roblox Premium subscription and the displayed **25% discount**. Use the amount shown in your server before confirming.

## When to use the shop

Use the gacha if you want to discover Fruitdex entries and can spare Gold for a random result. Check **Temp Fruits** when you want a specific power. One documented shop offer listed **Blaze** at **12,500 Gold**.

Compare abilities in the [Fruitdex](/en/fruits/) and historical rankings in the [Legacy tier list](/en/tier-list/) before spending.

## What to do after your first roll

Use the fruit in combat to develop mastery. Check the **Fruitdex** for a discovery reward under **Claim**, then test each ability’s range and cooldown on regular enemies. If the fruit becomes your main source of damage, build around **Vox Fruit**.
