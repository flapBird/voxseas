## What is Vox Seas?

Vox Seas is a pirate adventure RPG on Roblox, with island travel, story quests, weapons, fruit abilities and fighting styles. The current story begins with a festival on **Solmar Island**, followed by an investigation, a free Cutlass and your first sailing equipment.

New players can follow the [beginner route](/en/beginner/) and look up destinations in the [island map](/en/map/).

## What changed with the relaunch?

Older guides can use different names, prices or balance values. Our [fruit tier list](/en/tier-list/) labels historical rankings as Legacy. The [Fruitdex](/en/fruits/) follows the Dawn of Legends Trello, which calls the magma fruit Vulcanic. Elastic’s current rarity still needs confirmation. Poison and Kilo remain in the Legacy archive.

## Logia in the November balance update

The **November 15, 2026** balance update describes two conditions for Logia protection. You need to be at least 10 levels above the enemy, and the enemy must have no Vox Armor. Check both before using Logia in a fight.

## Where to check future updates

Start with [codes](/en/codes/) and their expiration status. After balance or content patches, check ability values, progression requirements and item stock. To find the channels linked by the developer, see the [Discord guide](/en/discord/) and [Trello guide](/en/trello/).
