## How to use the Navigator’s Map

Equip the **Navigator’s Map** and open **WORLD**. The green **YOU** marker indicates your position. Compare your destination with the compass and adjust course.

Use the quest marker during a story objective. When exploring freely, check the island names and coastal landmarks to confirm your location.

For your first crossing, follow **Supplies for the Crossing** to the shop behind Hale on Solmar. Claim the **Boat**, **Shipwright’s Hammer** and **Navigator’s Map** individually, equip them in your inventory and use the boat in open water. The [beginner guide](/en/beginner/) walks through the entire trip.

## Where to go first

### 1. Solmar Island

Start with Hango at the **Elder’s House** and complete the festival preparations. The Marine investigation earns you a free Cutlass. Solmar also has a Weapon Dealer, a Boat Seller and other starting services.

### 2. Forbidden Island

[Forbidden Island](/en/map/forbidden-island/) has Darius’s camp, Morgana’s Witch House and story encounters such as Veyrn. Set your spawn at the camp before making repeated trips between objectives.

### 3. Weapons in Circus Town and Solmar

The Arms Dealer in Circus Town sells the Dual Katana and Cutlass. Visit the dealer on Solmar Island for the Slingshot and Musket. See prices and abilities in the [weapon guide](/en/weapons/).

### 4. Harbor Island and Wandering Bastille

Both locations have Vox Gacha. [Harbor Island](/en/map/harbor-island/) also has a Weapons Dealer, the Old Explorer and a place to sell fish. Wandering Bastille is a restaurant ship with a spawn point and NPCs on deck. See directions and cooldown details in the [fruit rolling guide](/en/fruit-gacha/).

## Where to go after Rustfall

The island cards list **Prison (230–255)** and **Valoria (255–285)** after Rustfall. Later destinations are Windhaven (290–325), Vulcan Island and Ashforge Bastion (both 325–355), Marine Ford (355–400) and [Nerúvia (400–500)](https://trello.com/c/7TGdjFtM). Level 500 is the upper end of Nerúvia’s published island range. The card gives no character level cap.

## Returning to a discovered island

Find the **Mirror Keeper** near the spawn parrot. Choose an island you have already visited and check the fare in Gold. Sail there on your first visit to unlock the destination. Visiting an arena through the Boss Board does not unlock its island for return travel. [November 18 update](https://trello.com/c/q0rcGnIp).

## Training locations

- **Skull Island** has Askard by the campfire on high ground. Follow his Vox training instructions to the central well.
- **Rustfall** is the location listed on the [Raijin Kenpo fighting style card](https://trello.com/c/nllb8LE0).
- **Shark Park** has Nalu on the beach opposite the dock for Ryusui Ken training. Old Kaito stays on the dock.
- **Heavenstep Mountain** has seven checkpoint flags to activate on the way to the instructor at the summit.

## Tips for sailing

- Set your spawn whenever a new island offers that option.
- Bring enough resources before sailing somewhere specifically to buy an item.
- Match the map labels to your destination. Community nicknames can vary.
- Check level, key and party size requirements in the [boss guide](/en/bosses/).
- Look up the abilities and mastery of a new fruit in the [Fruitdex](/en/fruits/).
