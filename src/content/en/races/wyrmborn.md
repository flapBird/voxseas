## Wyrmborn’s bonuses

The [official Wyrmborn card](https://trello.com/c/aQO6yXkq) lists **5% higher damage** and **5% higher maximum health**. To put that into perspective, a 5% health increase takes a base of 1,000 to 1,050 before other gear effects. Check your stat panel for the value in your build.

## When to use Wyrmborn

Both bonuses help in activities that call for steady damage and survivability. Before returning to a Boss Room, consider what ended the last attempt. If you needed to survive one more hit or finish the fight after using your available moves, compare how the bonuses affect your current stats. Use the [boss guide](/en/bosses/) to check the entry requirements before making another attempt.

Some routes depend on travel time or recovery between fights. Compare [Beastborn](/en/races/beastborn/) for movement on land. If you already survive each fight and mainly need quicker health recovery, compare Angelborn in the [race directory](/en/races/).

## How to check the odds and price

Open **Character** and select the arrow beside your race portrait. The documented screen gives Wyrmborn an individual **10%** chance with **Crystals**. Each roll is random. Check the current price and odds, then compare the passive against the race your character already has. A reroll makes more sense when the bonus helps with activities you do regularly.
