## Beastborn passives

The [official Beastborn card](https://trello.com/c/KTd91LUe) documents two effects. Movement speed increases by **10%** and stamina regeneration by **5%**. The first helps you get to the next objective. The second helps recover stamina between crossings on foot and fights.

## Routes where Beastborn helps

Pick a land route you know well. Use the same start and finish, such as a quest NPC and the first group of enemies. Run the crossing with your usual gear and check the travel time and stamina you have left on arrival. If you often have to wait after spending stamina, also note the recovery time before you can start the next fight.

A route you repeat is a better comparison than a single sprint. For underwater routes, compare Tideborn’s breathing and swimming passives. For short encounters with little movement, compare [Wyrmborn’s](/en/races/wyrmborn/) direct damage and maximum health bonuses. The [island map](/en/map/) can help you pick comparable routes.

## How to check the reroll

Open **Character** and select the arrow beside your current portrait. The documented screen puts Beastborn in the Epic group, with a **22.5%** individual chance per Crystal roll. Check the current odds and price before you confirm. If you already have Beastborn, compare the effect in your character menu with the Trello entry.

See the [race directory](/en/races/) for all passives and the reroll steps.
