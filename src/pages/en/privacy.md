This policy explains how browsing the guide involves technical data, language preferences and third-party services.

## Browsing data

When you open a page, your browser sends its IP address and request details to the server to receive the content. The site loads Google Fonts and uses Google Tag Manager on the production domain. The tags configured in that service determine what additional data may be collected. Your language choice is stored locally in your browser.

## Google, third-party cookies and personalized ads

When Google ads are displayed, Google and other vendors may use cookies to select ads based on earlier visits to this and other sites. These identifiers can also help limit repeated ads and measure results. You can adjust personalization in [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/) and block third-party cookies in your browser. Contextual ads can still appear with personalization off. Read [how Google uses data from partner sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites?hl=en).

## Your rights under Brazil’s LGPD

You may request confirmation of processing, access, corrections and information about sharing. Under the conditions set by law, you may also request portability, anonymization, blocking or deletion. When processing relies on consent, you may ask what happens if you decline, withdraw consent and request deletion, subject to legal retention exceptions.

To exercise your rights, email [support@voxseas.online](mailto:support@voxseas.online). See the [contact page](/en/contact/) for guidance on making a request. You can also petition Brazil’s data protection authority. See the [ANPD guide to data subject rights (Portuguese)](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/assuntos/titular-de-dados-1/direito-dos-titulares).
