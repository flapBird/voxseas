Your browser saves your language preference under the local storage key `voxseas-language`. It persists between visits until you change languages or clear site data.

## Managing browser data

In your browser’s privacy settings, find the site data for voxseas.online and remove it to clear your language choice. Blocking third-party cookies is a separate browser setting. When ads are displayed, the [privacy policy](/en/privacy/) explains how to manage personalization.
