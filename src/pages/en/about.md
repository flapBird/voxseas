Vox Seas Brasil is an independent player guide to Vox Seas on Roblox. We publish in English and Brazilian Portuguese.

## Finding your way

If you just arrived in Solmar, start with the [beginner guide](/en/beginner/). Keep the [island map](/en/map/) handy for your next voyage or use the [Fruitdex](/en/fruits/) to compare abilities.

## Who writes the guide

This site's editors organize information for players. Roblox and the Vox Seas developers operate their own services. If a price, requirement or code has changed, see how to [report a correction](/en/contact/).
