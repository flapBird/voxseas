Email [support@voxseas.online](mailto:support@voxseas.online) with corrections or privacy requests.

To report an error in the guide, include the page address, the passage that needs correcting and what you saw in the game. For an expired code, include the code and the date you tried it.

## Privacy requests

Identify the right you want to exercise and the page or service involved. Please leave out passwords, identity documents and Roblox account details. Read our [privacy policy](/en/privacy/).
